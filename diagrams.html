Interactive Diagrams of Complete Architectures

Each diagram below is an expression written with PyNCD and drawn by TSNCD. The diagrams below include foundational, tutorial models such as standard attention variants. This builds up to classic models: Attention is All You Need, Mixtral-8x7B, and DeepSeek-V3. Finally, there are the modern models: GLM-5.3, and the absolute artwork which is DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, with all its mixed streams, batched indexers, and engram hashings.

There are three forms of diagrams: arrows and boxes, arrows and broadcasted, and all broadcasted. These go from how models are typically expressed without broadcasting information, through to the full NCD expressions which are akin to "seeing like a GPU". Different "views" of the same model are possible, and each is procedurally derived from the base form by the tools in PyNCD. A quantized abstract representation is sufficient to derive the unquantized form, the training algorithm, and the cached algorithm. You will also find a link to the notebooks which generates the models. So, go ahead and start exploring.
