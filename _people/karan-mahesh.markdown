Karan Mahesh is a MechE PhD student in the MIT-WHOI Joint Program. He works with Prof. Gioele Zardini and Dr. Michael Benjamin on game-theoretic frameworks for multi-agent marine autonomy. Prior to joining MIT, he received his BS in Mechanical Engineering from University of California, Berkeley and was an AI/ML Research Scientist at Aurora Flight Sciences.
